<p class="rp-lead">Airport turnarounds are coordinated operations involving equipment, personnel, and strict timing. This project studies how computer vision and decision models can support more reliable ground operations.</p>

<h2>Apron video analytics</h2>
<p>Through a Mitacs Accelerate collaboration with Aéroport AI, I develop and evaluate video-analytics systems for object tracking and event detection from fixed apron cameras, connecting perceptual models to time-sensitive operational decisions.</p>

<h2>Airport recovery simulation</h2>
<p>I also mentored a Mitacs Globalink aerospace researcher who built a multi-agent airport-turnaround simulator. I helped set the research direction and reviewed its implementation. On held-out simulated scenarios, the resulting recovery policy reduced 90th-percentile delay by <strong>19 minutes</strong> compared with rule-based recovery.</p>
