I am a Ph.D. student in Industrial Engineering at Concordia University, advised by [Prof. Yassine Yaakoubi](https://www.concordia.ca/faculty/yassine-yaakoubi.html). I study how learned decision systems should adapt when their environment changes: what new evidence can identify, how much of a model to revise, and when a local revision leads to better decisions. My work draws on causal inference, representation learning and operations research.

Before my Ph.D., I completed a Master of Computer Science at Concordia and McGill University, where I developed [MAGnet](/projects/magnet/) for MRI-to-CT synthesis, and a B.Sc. in Biology and Computer Science at McGill University.
